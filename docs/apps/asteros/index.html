Designed around the job

What AsterOS does

01

Know your server

See CPU, memory, network, storage and supported GPU statistics. Hardware and server plugins determine available readings.

02

Make apps your own

Arrange Docker apps in folders, choose custom icons, browse Community Applications, and review basic or advanced container settings.

03

Keep files and memories close

Browse your network shares and explicitly back up photos, videos and Live Photos to a chosen folder, with year/month organization and resumable progress.

Connections and boundaries

It works with what you choose.

Your Unraid server over HTTPS, SMB shares using your share account, and optional app-scoped Tailscale connectivity. The current direct connection workflow does not require an AsterOS Docker companion.

Nothing essential is bundled

AsterOS is in private TestFlight testing. External beta review is being prepared; no public invitation is available yet. Photo backup currently runs in the foreground after you start it. Use disposable files and containers for beta testing.

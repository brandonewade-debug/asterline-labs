CueForge

Shape ideas into reviewable lighting cues. CueForge explores how local models, saved-show inspection, and strict review gates can assist a lighting programmer without pretending drafts are verified console actions.

Designed around the job

What CueForge does

01

Inventory-aware planning

Ground drafts in imported fixture and preset information instead of free-form guesses.

02

Local model workflow

Run supported planning and review models on local hardware, with source material kept on the workstation.

03

Human approval boundary

Present expected cues, validation results, and known uncertainty before any operator-controlled next step.

Connections and boundaries

It works with what you choose.

Local model runtimes and saved ONYX show snapshots. Live console synchronization and native cue recording remain development work.

Nothing essential is bundled

CueForge is a prototype, not a production console adapter. Current validation does not establish safe autonomous programming or live-show reliability.

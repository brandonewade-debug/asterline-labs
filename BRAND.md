# Asterline Labs brand guide

## Core idea

The Asterline mark combines a forward-leaning **A**, an orbital path, and a guide star. It is intended to connect the studio’s media, live-production, networking, and systems work without locking the brand to one app or platform.

## Approved assets

Use the files in `docs/assets/brand/`:

| Asset | Use |
|---|---|
| `asterline-mark.svg` | Preferred mark for websites, documents, and scalable artwork |
| `asterline-mark-1024.png` | Transparent raster mark when SVG is unsupported |
| `asterline-lockup.svg` | Preferred horizontal mark plus Asterline Labs wordmark |
| `asterline-lockup.png` | Transparent horizontal raster lockup |

The SVG files are the source of truth. Do not trace a PNG when the vector is available.

## Primary colors

| Name | Hex | Purpose |
|---|---:|---|
| Aster cyan | `#67ECFF` | Orbit, connection, highlights |
| Signal blue | `#7BA9FF` | Primary gradient midpoint |
| Stellar violet | `#A56BFF` | Technology and depth |
| Space black | `#05070D` | Primary dark background |
| Starlight | `#F6F8FF` | Primary text on dark backgrounds |
| Muted steel | `#AAB4CB` | Secondary text |

The primary mark uses the cyan-to-blue-to-violet gradient. A one-color version may be used only when production limits require it; choose solid white on dark backgrounds or solid space black on light backgrounds.

## Clear space and minimum size

- Keep clear space around the mark equal to at least one quarter of the mark’s width.
- Keep clear space around the horizontal lockup equal to at least the height of the word `LABS`.
- Do not render the standalone mark below 24 CSS pixels wide in interface use.
- Do not render the horizontal lockup below 160 CSS pixels wide.
- At very small sizes, use the mark only and remove decorative glow rather than reducing legibility.

## Naming

Use **Asterline Labs** on first reference. **Asterline** may be used conversationally after the full name has appeared. Product attribution should read:

- Orbit by Asterline Labs
- Nova Stream by Asterline Labs
- Console Bridge by Asterline Labs
- CueForge by Asterline Labs
- DockerOS by Asterline Labs

Do not place a product name inside the Asterline mark.

## Do not

- Rotate, skew, stretch, or redraw the mark.
- Change the orbit into a complete circle or move the guide star.
- Apply drop shadows that obscure the orbit or counter of the letter A.
- Place the gradient mark on a busy image without adequate contrast.
- recolor individual app icons to make them look like the Asterline corporate mark.
- imply affiliation with Apple, Plex, Google, YouTube, OpenAI, MA Lighting, ONYX, Docker, Debian, or another third party.

## Trademark and name review

The visual identity is original project artwork, but this repository does not document a completed trademark clearance, business-name registration, or domain-availability review. Complete those checks before treating the name or mark as a registered or exclusive trademark.
